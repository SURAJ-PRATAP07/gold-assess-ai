# 🏆 GoldAssess AI

### AI-Powered Remote Gold Assessment for Digital Lending


## 🎯 Problem Statement

India holds over **25,000 tonnes of privately owned gold**. Traditional gold loan processes require:
- ❌ Physical branch visits
- ❌ Manual valuation by trained staff
- ❌ Specialized XRF machines
- ❌ Slow customer onboarding

## 💡 Our Solution

**GoldAssess AI** enables customers to:
1. 📸 Upload jewelry photos from home
2. 🎤 Record tap-test audio (optional)
3. 🤖 AI instantly provides pre-qualification estimate
4. 💰 Get loan eligibility without visiting a branch
